| Text | When | Where |
|---|---|---|
| `As a result of the agreed subject matter of advice, we have identified the scope of advice to be:` | `scope_items` given | First line of Agreed scope of advice |
| `- Review of your personal risk insurance needs: ` | Always, after the lead-in (default, Q-FF-2) | Second line of Agreed scope of advice |
| `- <agreed area>` | One per agreed advice area | Following lines |
| `TBC` | Fact that should be known was not said | Any cell. Highlighted yellow. |
| `Not disclosed` | A single item not disclosed | Any cell |
| `☑` / `☐` | Not applicable / Not disclosed box | Checkbox row of each section |
| `Good` | Health status, no condition disclosed (default, Q-FF-5) | Your health |
| `None` | Nothing said about incomplete or inaccurate information (default, Q-FF-6) | About our advice |
| `Yes` | Adviser expertise, nothing said (default, Q-FF-6) | About our advice |
| `No change` | Review client confirmed nothing changed in a section | Any changes box |
| `Total` | Added by the builder below income, expenses, assets, liabilities | Last table row |
| Advice limitations | Master's standard wording, never edited (default, Q-FF-17) | About our advice |

## Wording patterns (house examples, client details replaced)

- Why are you seeking advice: "[CLIENT_1], you would like to explore alternative options given the costs associated with your existing [INSURER_A] Income Protection policy."
- Goal: "Review Income protection coverage and implement a cost effective strategy moving forward in line with 70% of your income." | Short Term | [AMOUNT] per month | High
- Agreed scope areas: "Review of your income protection insurance." / "Implement a new cost-effective income protection strategy."

Never: an insurer or product as the outcome, "top up", "cancel", "Implementation of new ... cover of $X", "Cancellation of your ... policy", exclusion acknowledgements in goals.
