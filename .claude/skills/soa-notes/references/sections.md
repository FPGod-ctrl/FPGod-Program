# SOA Notes: what goes in each part

Write what the meeting actually established. Shorthand and bullets. Every gap in the body is `[ADVISER TO CONFIRM]` (default, Q-SH-3).

| Part | Content | Never |
|---|---|---|
| Brief (`Hey Howard,`) (default, Q-SN-3) | One line or bullet per cover to quote: cover type, life insured, sum insured or options (`[ADVISER TO CONFIRM]` if none set, Q-SN-1), IP waiting and benefit periods, inside or outside super (both if not discussed, Q-SN-8), stepped or level as discussed (otherwise `[ADVISER TO CONFIRM]`, Q-SN-15). Any research asked for (for example compare existing cover). Any insurer Tristan named to look at. | Sums or structures nobody discussed. |
| Documents to obtain | ☐ Existing policy schedules ☐ Super statements ☐ Quotes ☐ Risk Researcher run ☐ Pre-assessment for <condition> (one per disclosed condition, Q-SN-14) ☐ Comparison of existing against proposed cover, when existing cover could be replaced (default, Q-SN-7) | Items already on file. |
| Still to confirm with the client | ☐ One plain-words line per `TBC` in the fact find and meeting notes, de-duplicated (default, Q-SN-6). | Facts already known. |
| Financial circumstances | Shorthand: Incomes; Property & debt; Savings; Super; Estate. Annual gross, figures as in the fact find (default, Q-SN-10). | Long prose. |
| Goals | What the client wants the cover to do, in their terms (protect family, clear debt, replace income). | The recommended insurer, product or action (default, Q-SN-4). |
| Reasons for Seeking Advice | Why the client came: new need, life event, cost or value of existing cover, review. Referrer if known; referral fee `[ADVISER TO CONFIRM]` (default, Q-SN-11). | The recommendation (default, Q-SN-4). |
| Insurance Review and Cover Discussion | Existing cover held (insurer, type, amount, owner). What was discussed for each cover: options, the client's view, what the client said they value or do not value. | Product features from memory or general knowledge. |
| Cover Funding | How premiums were discussed being paid: super rollover, cash flow, split; stepped or level, if discussed. | A funding position the meeting did not reach. |
| Health Disclosure | Each condition: what, medication and dose, when diagnosed, whether controlled. Height and weight if given. Occupation details relevant to the rating. Or "No health conditions have been disclosed." (default, Q-SN-14) | Guesses about underwriting outcomes or loadings. |
| Advice and Recommendations | What was decided, cover by cover: new, retain, cancel, reduce, increase. In the firm's wording ("we recommend implementing...") (default, Q-SN-9). Everything open is `[ADVISER TO CONFIRM]`. Whether an INA was done is not recorded here; the SOA build asks (default, Q-SN-5). | Decisions not made. "Should". "Apply for". "Separate application". |
