Kids' Legacy Super & Insurance
As your children move into full-time work, it's critical — and something both I and our director, Ross Hennig, feel strongly about — that they take out Income Protection!
The reasoning is simple: if one of your kids suffered an unfortunate event without cover, the "bank of Mum and Dad" is effectively open to fund their lifestyle for the next 40-plus years. For a relatively small cost — roughly $1,000–$1,500 a year depending on their job, income and age — that risk sits with the insurer instead of eroding your own wealth. It's a no-brainer.
It's also worth flagging the government co-contribution: if the kids are earning between $1 and around $45,000 and you contribute $1,000 into their super, the government will add up to $500 on top — a great way to give their super an early boost. More importantly, it's a chance to start educating your children about money and investing — a genuine legacy item.
