**Your Wills & estate planning**
Your Wills are basic and haven't been looked at in years. Worth knowing there are two types: the basic Will, and a **comprehensive Will incorporating a testamentary trust**, where assets flow into a trust rather than directly into the beneficiary's hands. Two benefits: **tax** — income can be streamed to beneficiaries at adult marginal rates, including grandchildren under 18 (roughly $20,000 each, tax-free); and, more importantly in my view, **asset protection** — under a basic Will, if a relationship breaks down everything inherited is up for grabs. Under a testamentary trust it isn't in their hands, so it isn't on the table.

Because you're in Tasmania we can prepare Wills but not powers of attorney for other states, so I'd go back to your local lawyer and **ask directly for testamentary trust Wills** — most won't raise them with you. The litmus test: a basic Will runs about three pages, a comprehensive one closer to 23. In the order of $5,000, but do it once and it's done. Also worth flagging — **super doesn't follow your Will**, so we need valid beneficiary nominations in place.
