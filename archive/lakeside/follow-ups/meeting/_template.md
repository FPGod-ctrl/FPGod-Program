# Follow-up email — after a meeting ("Follow on from meeting")

**STATUS: TRAINED** (locked 2026-07-10 from 10 approved examples: Ornella & Eric,
Marie-Claire, Ana & Zane, Giulio, Claire, Carmen, Brendan & Julie, Brenden &
Candice, Adam, John & Meni).

**Gold-standard worked example** (a full Tristan-approved output, best single
reference for voice/length/structure): `examples/kirsty-JUL26.md`.

Client-facing email sent **after a client meeting/appointment** (in person, Zoom,
or a substantive call), typically generated **from the meeting transcript**.
SEPARATE type from the phone-call follow-up (`../phone-call/_template.md`) — that
one is warm/short and pushes to a booked meeting.

THIS one is a **detailed, substantive client email** — technically a summary of
the meeting, but a thorough one. It is NOT a terse recap: it walks through each
area in depth, with the specific figures, the reasoning, the trade-offs and the
"why", so the client can read it standalone and understand exactly where they
sit and what's been agreed. Err toward MORE detail, not less — the real examples
run many paragraphs per section. Reproduce this structure, depth and voice.

---

## Subject line

`FOLLOW ON FROM MEETING - [Client name(s)] [MMMYY]`

- All caps for "FOLLOW ON FROM MEETING", hyphen, then the client name(s), then
  month+2-digit-year with no space, e.g. `MAY26`.
- Example: `FOLLOW ON FROM MEETING - Brendan Mulvihill and Julie Gibson MAY26`
- This is a filing/record subject — do NOT reuse the phone-call "Great
  chatting…" style here.

## The fixed opening (always, in this order)

1. **Greeting.** `Hi [First],` — or `Hi [First] & [First],` for a couple, or
   `Hi [First] (& [First]),` when the second person was secondary/not present.

2. **Thank-you opener — personalised to the actual meeting.** One or two
   sentences. Name the setting and timing (came into the Brighton office / Zoom
   today / met last week / spoke yesterday morning) and, lightly, what was
   covered. Weave in ONE warm human note where there's a real hook —
   congratulations (engagement, home purchase, new baby), well-wishes (someone
   unwell), an apology (reschedule, or a delayed email), or "great to finally
   meet you in person." Never force it.
   - "Thank you so much for coming into the office to discuss your superannuation
     and retirement planning. It was great to finally meet you in person after
     our phone conversation a couple of weeks ago."
   - "It was great having you in at the Brighton office — thanks so much for
     making the time. And please do pass on our warmest wishes to Candice…"
   - "Thank you for taking the time to speak with us last week, and again,
     apologies for needing to reschedule a couple of weeks back."

3. **Bridge line — sets up the summary + actions.** One sentence, pick a variant:
   - "As discussed, I've set out below a summary of our conversation, along with
     key observations and the action items we agreed upon."
   - "Following our conversation, please find below a summary of what we
     discussed along with some action items."
   - "As promised, here's a summary of everything we covered, along with what's
     been agreed and what's coming next."
   - "Please see below a summary of our discussion and the key action items
     moving forward."

## The body — MODULAR themed sections (bold heading each)

Include ONLY the sections that were actually discussed, in this order. A
risk-only meeting may be just *Insurances → Action Items*; a full review runs
all of them. Every section is second-person ("you/your"), specific to their
numbers, and explains the **why** behind each point.

- **Current Financial Situation, Goals & Objectives** — (aliases used: "Current
  Situation", "Key Areas of Financial Situation", "Summary"). Their position:
  age(s), family/dependants, employment/income, assets (home value & mortgage,
  offset, super balances, shares, investment properties, cash), and their stated
  goals/timeframe. Prose or bullets. Fold personal/context notes in here.

- **Insurances** (or **Personal Insurances**) — current policies & insurer,
  quality assessment (call out legacy/grandfathered value: **agreed value**,
  **own-occupation**, "one income-producing duty of your own occupation",
  pre-Oct-2021 contracts that "cannot be replicated"), premium pressure and
  renewal timing, the proposed change and the trade-off, ownership/tax
  (personally owned vs inside super and deductibility). This is usually the
  meatiest section for a risk meeting.

- **Superannuation** — consolidation/active management, investment mix &
  de-risking near preservation age, TTR, **catch-up/carry-forward concessional
  contributions** (balance < $500k test, concessional cap **$32,500** this FY —
  NOT the old $30k), **downsizer** ($300k), non-concessional/carry-forward, and
  any **30 June** deadlines (flag processing lead time). Always: super doesn't
  follow the Will — needs a binding nomination.

- **Keep projections BRIEF.** This is a follow-on email, NOT a Strategy Paper or
  financial plan. Mention drawdown/retirement-income direction in a sentence or
  two ("your super should comfortably see you into your later years, with the
  Age Pension to supplement"); do NOT reproduce the year-by-year modelling —
  that's what the Strategy Paper is for. Say the exact figures will be mapped out
  there.

- **Financial Planning & Retirement** (aliases: "Strategy Discussion",
  "Financial Planning & Investment Strategy") — broader strategy, retirement
  income projection, structures. Where relevant, present the advice options with
  fees and **no-pressure framing**:
  - Strategy Paper — **$3,000 + GST** (key strategies, concise, no full
    modelling).
  - Full Financial Plan — **$6,000 + GST** (tax, investment, projections, super,
    estate/intergenerational; portion may be deductible investment advice).
  - TTR implementation/advice fee — **$2,500 + GST** (from the Carmen example).
  - Ongoing super management (if we take over the super via CFS) —
    **0.75% p.a. on the managed funds**.
  - Framing: "with no pressure either way", "we are genuinely happy for you to
    take your time."

- **Wills & Estate Planning** — basic Will vs **testamentary trust** Will;
  benefits = asset protection (creditor / relationship-breakdown / divorce),
  tax-effective income streaming to beneficiaries (incl. minors taxed at adult
  rates). Super passes by binding nomination, not the Will. Factor in parents'
  estates / expected inheritances. Offer to coordinate in-house solicitor
  **Doug**.

## Action Items (ALWAYS the final section, before sign-off)

Bold heading **Action Items** (or **Next Steps**). Bulleted or numbered. Blend
two kinds, and make the client's asks clear:

- **What I need from you:** confirm/update the attached **Client Profile**;
  send **super statement(s)**; review & confirm **health section** of the prior
  insurance application / complete **health questionnaire**; sign & return forms
  (photo/scan/text fine — no wet ink needed).
- **What I/we will do:** arrange the insurer/AIA call, obtain pre-assessment,
  prepare the **Strategy Paper / SOA** for review & signature, book a short
  10-min call to finalise. "Nothing is charged until the new cover is in force."

Bold the single most critical ask/figure where it helps it stand out.

## Wealth Locker (optional soft add-on section)

When relevant, add a short section offering **Wealth Locker** — the firm's new
app, a secure online place to store and manage the client's entire financial
world in one spot. Offer to set up an account and load their full position.
Light-touch, near the end, before Action Items.

## Optional close (softer meetings)

"If you have any questions at all in the meantime, please don't hesitate to
contact me. I look forward to hearing from you." Then the standard Lakeside

001313019 / Pareto Group Pty Ltd AFSL 418700. See [[adviser-ar-number]].

## Voice & compliance rules

- Warm, plain-English, second person. Openers: "As discussed", "We discussed",
  "We explored", "We also touched on", "We agreed", "Importantly", "Critically",
  "Worth flagging", "The key consideration is".
- **Frame recommendations as options to EXPLORE, not directives** — "one avenue
  worth exploring", "I'd suggest exploring a Strategy Paper", "my suggestion is
  to explore consolidating". Avoid "my recommendation is" / "you should". Keeps
  it a discussion summary, not personal advice.
- **Explain the why, but stay tight and personable** — cut lecturing tangents;
  every point should move the client's decision, and name their deeper
  motivation (e.g. "after a lifetime of hard work, you want more time with the
  grandkids"). Research house = **Lonsec**; in-house fund ~**10–20** underlying
  managed funds; reviews **six to twelve months**.
- **Hedge and stay compliant**: "may / potentially / generally", "assessed
  case-by-case by the insurer", "worth confirming with your accountant",
  "confirm your balance is below $500,000 on 1 July". This email is a **summary
  of discussion + observations, NOT personal advice** — formal advice is flagged
  as a coming step (Strategy Paper / **Statement of Advice** for review &
  signature). Position time-sensitive general points as items for the client to
  action "under your own validity". See [[soa-writing-style]].
- **Numbers come from Tristan / the transcript — never invent or free-hand
  maths.** Mirror the figures given; if something needs calculating or checking,
  flag it as "to confirm" rather than asserting. See [[plan-generator-calc-errors]].
- Bold used sparingly — key terms (agreed value, testamentary trust, downsizer)
  and critical action items/figures only.
- Firm = **Lakeside Financial**; Brighton office **Suite 201, Bay Street,
  Brighton VIC 3186**; in-house solicitor **Doug**.
- Do NOT copy draft artifacts seen in the source examples (e.g. an unfinished
  "…the main focus of our discussion was [blank]" line, or Brendan/Brenden
  spelling slips) — those were raw drafts, not the standard.
- If a doc is attached/requested, deliver per [[deliver-docs-to-downloads]].
