**Stepped vs level — and why it matters more for Jack and Haley**

Stepped starts cheap and climbs steeply past fifty. **Level** locks the rate at the age you start and holds it flat to 65. You pay more early on, but the two lines cross somewhere around year eight to twelve, and level is cheaper every year after that — by the back end, dramatically so. Two caveats I'd rather you heard from me than found in the fine print: insurers can still reprice an entire book, and indexation keeps creeping unless we switch it off.

The locked rate depends entirely on the age you start, so 26 versus 36 is a gap that never closes.
