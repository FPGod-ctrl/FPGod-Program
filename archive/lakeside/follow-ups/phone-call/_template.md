# Follow-up email — after a phone call

Standard format for the client email sent after an initial/outreach phone call
(Lakeside Financial). Approved format — reproduce this structure and voice.

Fill the `[bracketed]` placeholders. Keep the **critical action items** (Client
Profile + spouse super statement) bold and prominent. When you have the call
transcript + the client's data, write a **detailed, personalised recap** — never
generic.

---

**Subject:** Great chatting, [FirstName] — [Day Date Time] + a couple of things to send me

Hi [Name],

Thanks for taking my call today — really enjoyed the chat. As promised, here's a quick follow-on with a few pointers, the meeting details, and the couple of things I need from you and [Spouse] beforehand.

**Our meeting**
- **[Day Date] at [Time]**, in person at our Brighton office.
- **Address: Suite 201, Bay Street, Brighton VIC 3186** (we've moved just up the road).
- A calendar invite is on its way to your inbox.
- No charge for this meeting — it's simply a chance to see where we can add value.

**A recap of what we covered**

[One paragraph on their position — super balance(s), combined total, home value & mortgage, dependants / school-fee window, age vs the retirement window. Then a numbered list of the specific areas to work through:]

1. **Transition to retirement (from age 60).** [tax-free super environment; draw income while working; recontribution / tax savings; lead time.]
2. **Your investment mix.** [aggressive vs de-risking as retirement approaches.]
3. **Your current fund/platform.** [is it outdated / can it be positioned better.]
4. **[Spouse]'s super.** [defined benefit vs high-growth; unmanaged risk.]
5. **SMSF — only if relevant.** [worth it only to borrow to invest.]
6. **Wills & estate planning.** [basic vs comprehensive Will given asset size.]

The short version: [encouraging one-liner — they've done well; confident there's tax to save and structure to tighten before 60.]

**Two things I need from you before we meet** — if you can get to these this week it'd be ideal, as they let me prepare properly:

1. **Updated Client Profile** — attached. It's been a while, so it'll take a few minutes to bring your situation up to date (income, debts, goals, estate planning, etc.). Even confirming what's unchanged helps.
2. **[Spouse]'s super statement** — a copy of their most recent statement. I already have yours, so that's the only piece missing to complete the picture.

Between those two items and what I already hold on your account, I'll have everything I need to come prepared with some concrete options.

Any questions in the meantime, just give me a call on my mobile.
