**Your super account — a few things worth a conversation**

While I had your file open I took the opportunity to look through your latest statement. Nothing is wrong, and there's nothing urgent. But there are a couple of housekeeping items I'd like to go through with you properly when we next sit down.

**1. Your account is still in what's called "accumulation" phase.** This is the main one.

Accumulation is the phase your super sits in while you're still working and building it up. Once you're retired and past a certain age — and you're comfortably past it — you can move the account into what's called "pension" phase instead.

The difference matters because in accumulation, the earnings inside your account are taxed at 15%. In pension phase, they're generally not taxed at all. Your account earned around $4,600 over the past year, so we're talking in the order of several hundred dollars a year going to tax unnecessarily. Not a fortune, but it's your money, and it's been happening for some time.

The one thing to be aware of is that pension phase requires you to withdraw a minimum amount each year, and at your age that minimum is a higher percentage. So it isn't automatically the right answer if you'd rather leave the money untouched. It's a decision worth making properly rather than assuming — which is exactly why I'd like to talk it through with you rather than just do it.

**2. This money is available to you whenever you want it.** You're well past the age where super is locked away, so the balance is yours to draw on — in part or in full — whenever suits. You may have no need for it at all, and that's perfectly fine. I just want you to know the option is there.
