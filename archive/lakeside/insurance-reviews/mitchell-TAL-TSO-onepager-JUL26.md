**TAL TPD Support Option — what it means for your cover**

Prepared for Stephen Mitchell · TAL Accelerated Protection Policy 1790356 · July 2026

Hi Stephen,

TAL has written to you about a new option they can add to the Total & Permanent Disability (TPD) cover inside your super fund, called the **TPD Support Option (TSO)**. Their letter headlines it as *"same total amount, lower premiums."* Here's what it actually involves and where we land on it.

**What TAL is offering**

- You can choose to add TSO to your existing TPD cover. You don't have to — if you do nothing, your cover stays exactly as it is.
- TAL says adding it *may* reduce the premium on your TPD cover (they don't quote a figure, and any saving isn't guaranteed — they can change it later).

**What actually changes if you add it**

- The total amount of your cover stays the same. What changes is **how it gets paid on certain claims**.
- For claims caused by **mental health conditions, fatigue or functional disorders** (things like depression, anxiety, chronic fatigue, fibromyalgia), TAL would no longer pay you one lump sum. Instead they'd pay **20% a year, over at least four years (up to five payments)** — and you'd have to still qualify as permanently disabled at each yearly review to receive the next instalment.
- For **all other claims** — a physical injury, cancer, a stroke — nothing changes; you'd still receive a single lump sum as you do now.
- The option ends at age 65 regardless.

**Our view**

- This isn't better cover — it's **cheaper cover in exchange for a weaker payout** on exactly one group of conditions (mental health and fatigue-related), which happen to be among the most common TPD claims today.
- The trade you'd be making is a possible premium saving now against giving up the certainty of a lump sum if you ever needed to claim for one of those conditions — money you might need up front to clear debt, adjust your lifestyle or take the pressure off while you recover.
- The saving also isn't locked in, whereas the change to your payout would be.

**What we suggest**

- There's no rush and no action needed to keep things as they are. Our starting position is to **leave your TPD cover unchanged**.
- If reducing premiums is a priority for you, we're happy to request the exact TSO quote from TAL so we can see the real saving and weigh it up together — rather than deciding on a "may cost less."
