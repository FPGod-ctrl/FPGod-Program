## 2. Replacement-product comparison — super switch (s947D)

Triggered **only if** the clients choose Option (b): transition from Aware Super (John) / Australian Super (Meni) to **CFS FirstChoice Wholesale Personal Super — Lakeside Core Balanced model**. Required disclosures:

| s947D item | Detail to document | Source / status |
|---|---|---|
| Charges to **dispose of / reduce** the existing interest | Exit/withdrawal fees, buy-sell spreads, CGT (nil — super-fund level), any deferred costs | **[TO CONFIRM]** with Aware / Australian Super |
| Charges to **acquire** the new interest | CFS admin 0.20% + ICR ~0.38% + transaction ~0% = ~0.59% product; **+ 0.75% adviser fee** (→0.50% >$1M); all-in ~1.30% | SOA §16 / Appendix B — **re-run calculator at combined/post-contribution balances** |
| **Benefits lost** by switching | **CRITICAL: John's in-fund insurance (Death/TPD $28,938, IP $1,000/mo) is irreplaceable given his cancer diagnosis** — a rollover could cancel it. Meni's cover (Death/TPD $230,000, IP $4,000/mo) | SOA §15 — **do NOT cancel any cover until replacement confirmed in force; likely retain John's Aware account regardless** |
| Other significant consequences | Performance: Lakeside model has **not** out-performed the current industry options at higher cost (SOA §16); investment-option change inside existing fund achieves the de-risk without a switch | SOA §9, §16, §19 |

**Recommendation already documented:** de-risk within existing funds is the cheaper, defensible default; the switch must be justified by advice/service value the clients positively choose — not cost or returns. Conflict disclosed (§4).
