**Why your premium has climbed**

Three things are working against you on this policy, and it's worth understanding them because they shape what we do next.

1. **Stepped premiums.** Your cover is priced on what's called a stepped basis, which means the premium rises every year as you get older. That's standard across the market, but it bites harder each year.
2. **Indexation.** Your level of cover creeps up automatically each year to keep pace with inflation. Useful in principle, but you're paying for a little more cover every year whether you need it or not.
3. **The age of the policy — and this is the critical one for you.** You took this out back in **2014**. The longer you stay on one policy with the same insurer, the more they tend to hike the rates. They work on the assumption that you won't move. Unlike a home loan, where you can ring the bank and negotiate, insurers aren't nearly as accommodating. Which is how you end up at **$467.02**.
