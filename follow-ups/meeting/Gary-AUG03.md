**Income protection versus TPD — the difference you asked about**

- **TPD** — a lump sum, paid only if you can never work again in your own or a reasonably suited occupation. Difficult to claim.
- **Income protection** — the catch-all. Injury, illness or sickness leaving you unable to work *now*. That might be six months, eighteen months, or permanently through to 65. Pays a monthly benefit after your 90-day waiting period.

They aren't alternatives. If you were ever deemed totally and permanently disabled, you'd receive the TPD lump sum *and* continue receiving the monthly income protection benefit through to 65. Cancelling TPD gives up the lump sum; it doesn't touch the income stream.
