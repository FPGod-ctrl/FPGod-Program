**Wills & estate planning**
This is the area I'd most encourage you to close off — you've put it off, and with one son, a home and growing super, it matters:

- Be aware of the **super death tax**: where super passes to non-dependent adult children, tax applies to the taxable component — a cost many don't see coming, which careful nominations and structuring can manage.
- I'd recommend **comprehensive Wills incorporating a testamentary trust**. A testamentary trust can only be created through a Will (and only takes effect on death), and it delivers two big benefits: **tax** (income can be streamed to beneficiaries — including children/grandchildren — at adult marginal rates) and, more importantly in my view, **asset protection** (shielding what's inherited if a relationship ever breaks down, across generations).
