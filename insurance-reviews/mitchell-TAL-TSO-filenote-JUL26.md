## Summary of the offer

TAL has made the TPD Support Option available on the client's Accelerated Protection TPD cover. TSO is an opt-in variation to the **payment structure** of the TPD benefit — not additional cover. Marketed as "same total amount, lower premiums." No action is required to maintain existing cover; TSO applies only if elected and accepted by TAL (no new underwriting to opt in).

## What TSO changes

- Applies only to claims caused by **TPD Support Conditions**: mental health conditions/disorders, fatigue and functional disorders (chronic fatigue, fibromyalgia, functional neurological disorder, long COVID, burnout), and specific syndromes (POTS, post-concussion, vestibular). Excludes Alzheimer's/dementia.
- For those conditions, benefit is paid as **up to 5 partial payments of 20%, 12 months apart, over a minimum of 4 years**, with the client required to satisfy the TPD definition (Part B) at **each annual review** to receive each instalment.
- For those conditions, the **any-occupation-style TSO definition applies** even where the base policy is Own Occupation.
- All other conditions (physical injury, cancer, etc.) continue to pay a **single lump sum** unchanged.
- Ancillary: Inflation Protection ceases once first Initial Support Benefit paid; benefit reduces by amounts paid; premium waived 12 months after each payment; if ≤$100k remaining after 4+ payments, remainder paid as lump sum; age 62+ at disablement or ADL definition → paid as lump sum; option ends at policy anniversary before age 65.

## Assessment

- TSO is **not enhanced cover**. On physical/catastrophic claims it equals existing cover; on psychological/fatigue claims it is **inferior for the insured** — instalments in place of a lump sum, plus annual re-qualification risk (recovery before a review date stops further instalments).
- The premium saving is **unquoted and non-guaranteed** ("may cost less," TAL reserves the right to vary). The client would be trading a locked-in payout downgrade for an unlocked, potential premium reduction.
- Mental-health conditions are among the largest and growing TPD claim categories, so the change targets exactly the band where claim likelihood is rising.
- Base-definition note: if the underlying TPD is Own Occupation, TSO effectively substitutes an any-occ test for these conditions — a material downgrade for a professional. **To confirm: current base TPD definition on policy 1790356.**
- Cover is held via super, so the SIS permanent-incapacity test already overlays any TPD claim; TSO does not change this.

## Recommendation / position

- **Default position: leave TPD cover unchanged (do not elect TSO).** No action preserves existing cover.
- If premium reduction is a client priority, request the specific TSO quote for policy 1790356 to quantify the saving, then reassess against the payout trade-off before any change.
