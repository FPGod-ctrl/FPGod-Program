# House wording (verbatim)

Reproduce these blocks exactly. Change only names, pronouns and plurals ("you" / "you both",
"policy" / "policies"). Do not paraphrase them. Do not drop one because it reads as
boilerplate. Do not add a block that does not apply to the client (each block says when it
applies).

Sources: the firm wording file (blocks found in most of 32 completed Legacy SOAs; the count in
brackets is how many of the 32 contain it) and the adviser's own corrections (marked
"adviser"). Typos in the source are kept and flagged; do not fix them until answered.
[Q-SOA-13]

Placeholders: `<Name>` = client first name(s); `[INSURER_A]` etc.

## INA — Insurance needs analysis (section 6)

Applies when the adviser answers **no** to "Was a full INA done?" [Q-SOA-1]

1. (objectives.2, 32): "During our discussions, you indicated a range of goals and objectives that you are seeking to achieve in relation to your wealth protection. After a detailed discussion you were very clear about the level and types of cover that you want to meet your specific goals and objectives."
2. (adviser; firm file needs-analysis-position.1 omits "you"): "As you were very specific about your insurance needs you declined to undertake a full Insurance Needs Analysis. Your goals are very specific and you have provided reasoning as to how the insurance recommendation was determined."
3. **Note** (adviser): "<Name>, please note that the insurance cover amounts outlined in this document have been based on the amounts you have requested. These cover levels are considered client-directed and reflect your expressed preferences."
4. (adviser) "You have directed the following:" then one bullet per action. A template, not fixed text. Example shape:
   - Cancel your existing [INSURER_B] income protection policy [POLICY_NO] ([AMOUNT] per month, [WAITING] waiting period, benefit period to age [AGE]).
   - Implement new income protection cover of [AMOUNT] per month to age [AGE] with a [WAITING] day waiting period.
   - Retain your existing [INSURER_C] income protection of [AMOUNT] per month with no change.
   - Total income protection cover: [AMOUNT] per month.
   - Then one line naming the covers that were not reviewed.
5. **Incomplete INA warning** (needs-analysis-position.2, 32): "We have recommended that you complete a full insurance needs analysis to determine the appropriate level of cover to meet your relevant circumstances, however you have declined this advice. As a result, you may be over or underinsured, and should you receive a claim it may not meet your required needs and objectives."

When an INA **was** done: no house wording exists. [Q-SOA-1]

  - "Access to the recommended level of cover is subject to underwriting requirements and acceptance by the life insurance company." (also underwriting.5, 23)
  - "Initial and ongoing premiums apply in relation to your insurance policy."
- Example risk bullets (adviser), use only where they apply:

  - Trauma (general.24, 22): "For a trauma claim to be paid, the diagnosis must meet the policy definition of the critical illness that is the subject of the claim."
- Feature / benefit lines available (use where they apply):
  - Trauma (general.28, 21): "Trauma (or Critical Illness) cover is designed to pay you a lump sum in the event that you suffer a specific illness or injury, such as heart attack or stroke, that may not leave you disabled long term but is traumatic at the time of occurrence. ## Never use

- "duty of disclosure" (superseded). Use "duty to take reasonable care not to make a misrepresentation".
- "This policy should be selected with the following features:"
- "Your cover should be owned in your own name."
- "apply for", "you should apply", "we recommend you apply", "separate application".
- "(self owned)" or any ownership tag in a heading.
- "No fees are payable to cancel your [INSURER_B] policy".
- Template label "Benefits" (use the better-off label) or "Considerations" alone.
- The long XPLAN auto-generated benefit sentences (e.g. "Able to return to work multiple times…").
- general.1 ("…personal and investment objectives"), general.12 ("Several steps are involved in designing a strategy…"), general.23 ("increase the size of your estate…") — investment or estate wording not used in a risk-only SOA. [Q-SOA-13]
- The garbled template fragment general.17 ("Life Cover Buy Back (Tr)Required buy back period…").
- Any wording that names a level-premium crossover year: none is approved. [Q-SOA-12]
