# Recommendation sections

One section per step in the Strategy overview, in the same order, with the same heading
text as the step. Every section has the five parts below. Placeholders: `[CLIENT_1]`,
`[CLIENT_2]`, `[INSURER_A]` (recommended), `[INSURER_B]` (existing, being replaced or
cancelled), `[INSURER_C]` (existing, retained), `[PRODUCT]`, `[AMOUNT]`, `[POLICY_NO]`.

## The five parts

| # | Part | Label in the SOA |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Recommendation | Section heading + recommendation paragraph |
| 2 | Why you are likely better off | "Why you are likely better off with our advice" [Q-SOA-5] |
| 3 | Features: levels of cover and features | "This policy will have the following levels of coverage & following features:" |
| 4 | Risks, considerations and consequences | "Risks, Considerations & Consequences" [Q-SOA-5] |
| 5 | Other things to consider / exclusion acknowledgements | Separate paragraph after the features list [Q-SOA-6] |

Default order on the page, until answered: 1 → 3 → 5 → 2 → 4 (the firm layout puts the
features list straight under the recommendation and the acknowledgement straight after the
features; the two boxes follow). If the clone base orders them differently, keep its order.
[Q-SOA-6]

## Wording rules for every section

- Heading: the cover type or action, no ownership tag. "Income protection insurance", not
  "Income protection insurance (self owned)".
- Verb: "we recommend implementing a new [INSURER_A] [PRODUCT] policy". Never "apply".
- No "should" in the recommendation or features lead-in.
- Never "separate application".
- Ownership is stated in the features list, not the heading.
- Use only facts from the file. Unknown → `[ADVISER TO CONFIRM]`.

## A. New cover

**1. Recommendation.** One paragraph addressed by first name:
"[CLIENT_1], we recommend implementing a new [INSURER_A] [PRODUCT] policy with [cover] of
[AMOUNT] [owned by / held through …]." Give the reason in one sentence tied to the goal in
"Your objectives".

**3. Features.** "This policy will have the following levels of coverage & following
features:" then a list:
- Each cover and sum insured (per life insured for a couple).
- IP: monthly benefit, waiting period, benefit period, benefit type (agreed / indemnity where
  on the quote), key options.
- TPD: definition (own / any occupation), and whether linked or SuperLinked.
- Trauma: level (standard / premier etc., from the quote), options.
- Premium structure (stepped or level) and frequency. [Q-SOA-12]
- Owner and how the premium is paid (personal cash flow, super rollover). [Q-SOA-11]
- Optional lead-in `recommendations.1` and close `recommendations.2`. [Q-SOA-6]

**5. Other things to consider / exclusion acknowledgements.** A separate paragraph after the
features list, only where the file supports it: an expected exclusion, loading or term from a
pre-assessment, framed as the client's acceptance ("You have acknowledged that the new policy
will likely carry the same [CONDITION] exclusion…"). No label like "Lower back exclusion:".

**2. Why you are likely better off with our advice.** Bullets, in this order:
1. A client-named lead bullet with the headline gain: "[CLIENT_1] and [CLIENT_2], the
   recommendation provides…". Keep the cost comparison specific ($ per month and the saving)
   where there is one.
2. An insurer-specific feature, only if the research on file supports it (e.g. a profit-share
   or loyalty feature shown in the Risk Researcher run).
3. What the cover type does for the client.
4. A financial-security bullet: debts, lifestyle, family (only what is true for the client).
   [Q-SOA-13]
5. Structure and ownership points (e.g. premium funded from super to ease cash flow;
   personal ownership keeps own-occupation TPD or trauma available).
For each sum insured, give the reason against the client's debts, income, dependants or
stated goal. Reasoning, not arithmetic.

**4. Risks, Considerations & Consequences.** Short bullets, one or two sentences each, cause
and consequence. Only the points that apply to this cover. Examples:
- Personally owned Life / TPD / Trauma: "The insurance premiums are not tax deductible. In
  return the claim proceeds will be paid to you tax-free." [Q-SOA-11]
- IP outside super: premiums generally tax-deductible; benefits are taxable income. [Q-SOA-11]
- TPD: "To qualify for a TPD payment you will need to satisfy the definition(s)…"
- Stepped premiums rise each year with age. [Q-SOA-12]
- Inside super: premiums reduce the retirement balance; benefit paid to the trustee first,
  then subject to a condition of release and tax rules. [Q-SOA-11]
- Replacement-only bullets do **not** appear on pure new cover. [Q-SOA-4]
Always end with, in order:
- "Access to the recommended level of cover is subject to underwriting requirements and acceptance by the life insurance company."
- "Initial and ongoing premiums apply in relation to your insurance policy."

## B. Replace existing cover

A replacement is two strategy steps plus a replacement advice block:
1. The **new cover** section (A above), for the new policy.
2. A **Cancel** section (D below) for the old policy, "once the new cover is in force".
3. A **Replacement advice** block for each replaced policy.

Add to the new-cover Risks box (only on replacement): suicide exclusion (`replacement.1`, only
where Life replaces Life), non-disclosure period (`replacement.2`), continuity
(`replacement.5`) and do-not-cancel-early (`general.21`). [Q-SOA-4]

**Replacement advice block.**
- Existing vs recommended table: insurer, product, owner, covers and sums insured, waiting and
  benefit periods, definitions, premium structure, annual premium, known exclusions or
  loadings. [Q-SOA-4]
- **Like-for-like comparison** priced at the **recommended** cover levels. If the existing
  policy or fund cannot offer some cover (e.g. trauma, own-occupation TPD), strip that cover
  out of the recommended figure so both sides compare the same cover. Use the existing
  insurer's or fund's own cost-of-cover figure where on file.
- "Differences in Benefits - IMPROVEMENTS:" — bullets that are just the Risk Researcher
  provision names where the Compare Ratings / One on One Comparison rating is better for the
  recommended policy.
- "Differences in Benefits - DECREASES:" — provision names where it is worse.
- Several existing insurers → group the bullets under each existing insurer's name.
- "Other Note: A more extensive listing of comparable benefits is available on file"
- **Costs of replacement:** only "The new [INSURER_A] policy is subject to underwriting, which
  may include a medical examination, blood test and financial evidence of your income."
- Review list: the XPLAN replacement step behind this table.

## C. Retain existing cover

Heading: "Retain your existing [INSURER_C] [cover] cover".

1. **Recommendation:** one short paragraph — "We also discussed your existing [INSURER_C]
   [cover] cover. You advised that you are satisfied with this cover and do not wish to
   review it at this time. As a result, we have not recommended any changes." Or "You have
   directed to retain your existing [INSURER_C] [cover] cover with no changes:" plus the
   cover amounts.
3. **Features:** the cover amounts list (if not already in the paragraph). No new features.
5. **Other things to consider:** only if the file raises one.
2. **Better off:** "Your existing level of cover continues to be consistent with your needs.
   You have indicated you are comfortable with the level of cover." plus one feature bullet
   if relevant (e.g. Agreed Value). [Q-SOA-5: the firm examples label this box "Why are you
   likely better off with our recommendations"; default is the standard label]
4. **Risks:** the two fixed lines: "There has been no change to the levels of cover and/or
   other terms of your personal risk insurance policy(ies)." and "If there are changes to your
   personal situation… we will reassess…" (full sentence from the clone base). Not the two
   underwriting closing lines: nothing is being underwritten.

## D. Cancel existing cover

Heading: "Cancel your existing [INSURER_B] [cover] policy". Shape not settled. [Q-SOA-18]
Default:

1. **Recommendation:** "We recommend cancelling your existing [INSURER_B] [cover] policy
   [POLICY_NO] once your new [INSURER_A] cover is confirmed in force." (Cancel-only: drop the
   "once…" clause and give the reason tied to the client's goal.)
3. **Features:** what is being given up: cover amounts, premium, any feature not replaced.
5. **Other things to consider:** benefits lost that the new policy does not have (from
   DECREASES).
2. **Better off:** the saving or the reason (premium, value, duplication), specific in $.
4. **Risks:**
   - Health check `replacement.4`.
   - Replacement: do not cancel until the new policy is in force (`replacement.5`,
     `general.21`).
   - Loss of any benefit listed under DECREASES.
   - "Initial and ongoing premiums apply…" is not used: no premium continues.

The pack carries a dated cancellation letter; that is the pack skill's job.

---

## E. Reduce existing cover (alteration)

Shape not settled. [Q-SOA-18] Default: as A, with the verb "we recommend reducing your
existing [INSURER_C] [cover] from [AMOUNT] to [AMOUNT]", the features list showing the new
levels, the yearly saving in the better-off box, and the health check `replacement.4` in the
Risks box. The clawback wording applies if the policy was placed within two years. Review
list: the insurer's signed alteration quote is the instrument.

## F. Increase existing cover (alteration)

Not found in any firm source. [Q-SOA-18] Default: as A, with "we recommend increasing your
existing [INSURER_C] [cover] from [AMOUNT] to [AMOUNT]", the increase subject to underwriting,
and the yearly premium increase stated. Mark the section `[ADVISER TO CONFIRM]` for review.
