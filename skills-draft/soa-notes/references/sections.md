# SOA Notes: what goes in each part

Write what the meeting actually established. Shorthand and bullets. Anything not decided is `[ADVISER TO CONFIRM]`.

| Part | Content | Never |
|---|---|---|
| Brief (`Hey Howard,`) | One line or bullet per cover to quote: cover type, life insured, sum insured or options, IP waiting and benefit periods, inside or outside super, stepped or level if discussed. Any research asked for (for example compare existing cover). Any insurer Tristan named to look at. | Sums or structures nobody discussed. |
| Documents to obtain | ☐ Existing policy schedules ☐ Super statements ☐ Quotes ☐ Risk Researcher run ☐ Pre-assessment for <condition> (one per disclosed condition) | Items already on file. |
| Still to confirm with the client | ☐ One plain-words line per `TBC` in the fact find and meeting notes, de-duplicated. | Facts already known. |
| Financial circumstances | Shorthand: Incomes; Property & debt; Savings; Super; Estate. Figures as in the fact find. | Long prose. |
| Goals | What the client wants the cover to do, in their terms (protect family, clear debt, replace income). | The recommended insurer, product or action. |
| Reasons for Seeking Advice | Why the client came: new need, life event, cost or value of existing cover, review. Referrer if known. | The recommendation. |
| Insurance Review and Cover Discussion | Existing cover held (insurer, type, amount, owner). What was discussed for each cover: options, the client's view, what the client said they value or do not value. | Product features from memory or general knowledge. |
| Cover Funding | How premiums were discussed being paid: super rollover, cash flow, split; stepped or level, if discussed. | A funding position the meeting did not reach. |
| Health Disclosure | Each condition: what, medication and dose, when diagnosed, whether controlled. Height and weight if given. Occupation details relevant to the rating. Or "No health conditions have been disclosed." | Guesses about underwriting outcomes or loadings. |
| Advice and Recommendations | What was decided, cover by cover: new, retain, cancel, reduce, increase. In the firm's wording ("we recommend implementing..."). Everything open is `[ADVISER TO CONFIRM]`. | Decisions not made. "Should". "Separate application". |
