**The main thing: you have no beneficiary nomination.** Your statement says plainly that no beneficiary details have been provided.

Superannuation does **not** pass under your Will — the fund handles it separately. Where there's no nomination, the trustee decides who receives it, based on their own assessment of your circumstances. That takes time, and it may not land where you'd want it to.

Your balance is **$33,583**. Sorting the nomination takes about five minutes and costs nothing. It's the single most worthwhile thing you could do with this account.
