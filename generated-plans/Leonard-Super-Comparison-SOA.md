## 8. Insurance — the most important risk in this switch  [CRITICAL]

**Switching funds can cause you to lose the insurance you currently hold.** Inside your current fund you have **Death $211,200, TPD $105,600, and Income Protection $5,198/month** — valuable cover that **ceases if your account is closed**.

**Rules to follow — do not skip:**
- **Do NOT cancel or roll out of your current fund until replacement cover in the new fund is confirmed accepted and in force.**
- New cover in CFS FirstChoice will be **underwritten** — if your health, occupation or pastimes have changed since the current cover started, the new cover could be **declined, loaded, or have exclusions**. Compare the **type, amount, definitions, waiting/benefit periods and cost** of the replacement against your current cover.
- If equivalent replacement cover **cannot** be obtained, **retaining the current fund (or its insurance) may be the better course** — flag this before proceeding.

*Legislative basis: Corporations Act s947D (benefits lost on replacement); SIS Act 1993 (insurance in super).*
